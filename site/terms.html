4. Seats and requests

Seats are the people who sign in through single sign-on or are provisioned through SCIM. Your plan sets how many. Control Tower won't let more sign in, and you can add seats at any time.

Requests a year is the number of calls through the gateway that your plan includes each license year. Control Tower counts them and shows you the total. Going over never slows or stops traffic and is never charged automatically. If you regularly go over, we'll agree the right capacity with you at renewal.

7. When a subscription ends

Your license key keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. Then there is a 14-day grace period. After it:

  • the Enterprise features stop;
  • password sign-in works again, so nobody is locked out;
  • gateway traffic is never affected, and the open-source features keep working;
  • your data stays where it always was: on your servers.
